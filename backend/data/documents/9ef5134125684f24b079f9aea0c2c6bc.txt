SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Service Agreement is entered into on 1st March 2026 between:
TechCorp India Pvt Ltd ("Client") and DataWorks Solutions ("Provider").

Section 1 — Scope of Services
The Provider shall deliver software development and AI consulting services.
All deliverables must comply with Schedule A specifications.
The Provider shall assign a dedicated project manager for this engagement.

Section 2 — Payment Terms  
The Client shall pay INR 5,00,000 per month within 30 days of invoice.
Late payments attract 2% monthly interest on outstanding amounts.
Invoices must be submitted on the first working day of each month.

Section 3 — Confidentiality
Both parties shall maintain strict confidentiality of proprietary information.
This obligation survives termination for a period of three years.
Disclosure to third parties requires prior written consent.

Section 4 — Termination
Either party may terminate with 60 days written notice.
Immediate termination is permitted for material breach or insolvency.
Upon termination, all company data must be returned within 14 days.

Section 5 — Governing Law and Dispute Resolution
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India.
Disputes shall be resolved through arbitration under the Arbitration Act 1996.
The seat of arbitration shall be Bangalore, Karnataka.

Section 6 — Liability
Neither party shall be liable for indirect or consequential damages.
Total liability is capped at three months of fees paid under this Agreement.

WHEREAS the parties have executed this Agreement as of the date written above.
