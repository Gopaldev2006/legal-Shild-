SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Service Agreement is entered into on 1st April 2026 between:
GlobalTech Solutions Pvt Ltd ("Client") and CodeWorks India ("Provider").

Section 1 — Scope
The Provider shall deliver software development, AI integration, and data analytics.
All deliverables must meet the specifications outlined in Schedule A.

Section 2 — Payment
The Client shall pay INR 8,00,000 per month within 30 days of invoice.
Late payments attract 1.5% monthly interest on outstanding amounts.
Invoices shall be submitted on the first working day of each month.

Section 3 — Confidentiality
Both parties agree to keep all proprietary information strictly confidential.
This obligation shall survive termination for a period of two years.
Disclosure to third parties requires prior written consent.

Section 4 — Termination
Either party may terminate with 60 days written notice.
Immediate termination is permitted in cases of material breach or insolvency.
Upon termination all company assets must be returned within 14 days.

Section 5 — Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India.
Disputes shall be resolved through arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act 1996.
The seat of arbitration shall be Bangalore, Karnataka.

WHEREAS the parties have duly executed this Agreement on the date above.
